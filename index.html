How we calculate

The rules behind every number on this site.

Which shift does a reading belong to?

Shift 1 runs 07:00–15:00, shift 2 15:00–23:00 and shift 3 23:00–07:00 the next day. Gas for a shift is read off the totalizers at those boundaries. A gas weight check written before 07:00 counts toward the previous day's shift 3. A shift shows "no meter data" when the readings don't cover it.

How does the meter's scf become pounds?

The blender reports standard cubic feet for each gas. One scf of N₂O weighs 0.116 lb and one scf of N₂ weighs 0.0739 lb. The blend ratio is by volume, so a blend set to 85% N₂O by volume is 89.9% N₂O by mass.

Where does each shift's target come from?

In order of preference: the average of that line, date and shift's paper gas weight checks; then Enact's operating gas weight for that shift (gas-blend items only); then the items' BOM gas per can. The table's Basis column says which one was used.

What does waste % mean?

Waste % = (gas metered − target) ÷ target. Target = cans filled × target grams per can. 0% means the meter matches what went into the cans; 50% means half as much gas again went somewhere other than a can.

How is efficiency worked out?

Cans produced ÷ the cans the filler could make at its setpoint over the shift window (C Line 300 cans a minute, D Line 250). Cases are converted to cans with each item's EQV, so a 6-pack case counts 6 cans.

What counts as a gas event?

Times the blend system was open with no gas-blend production: startup before the first can (expected), downtime stops of 30 minutes or more, end of schedule including CIP, and changeovers to non-gas items. The last three are the opportunity, because closing the blend would have saved that gas outright.

Items

Gas standards per item from the BOM (lb per case) and the same figures per can, which is what every calculation on the site uses. Ratio is N₂O by volume as the blender delivers it; the BOM's own N₂O / N₂ split is shown as a mass ratio for comparison.

Per-can figures = lb/case × 453.592 ÷ cans per case. When "BOM split (mass)" matches the ratio column numerically, the BOM was written by mass while the blender runs by volume. Hover a code for its notes.

scf to weight

What does the meter's number weigh?

The blender reports standard cubic feet. One scf of N₂O is 0.116 lb; one scf of N₂ is 0.0739 lb. Enter a quantity in any unit and the calculator gives the others. For a blend, it also splits the total between the two gases using the volume ratio the blender runs.
