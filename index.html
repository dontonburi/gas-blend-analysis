Waste % = gas metered over the target: the target is the average gas weight from the paperwork (gas weight checks) for that line, date and shift when it exists, otherwise the items' BOM standard. Efficiency = cans produced ÷ cans the filler could make at setpoint (C 300 cpm, D 250 cpm) over the shift window. Shifts: 1 = 07:00–15:00, 2 = 15:00–23:00, 3 = 23:00–07:00 next day. Gas per shift is read off the totalizers at the shift boundaries; a shift shows “no meter data” when readings don't cover it.

Conversions

The meter reports standard cubic feet (scf) — a mass measurement expressed as the volume the gas would occupy at 60°F and 14.7 psia. These are the factors the dashboard uses.

Gaslb per scfg per scfscf per lbscf per kg
N₂O (nitrous oxide, 44.01 g/mol)0.116052.68.6219.0
N₂ (nitrogen, 28.01 g/mol)0.073933.513.529.8

A ratio of scf between the two gases is a ratio by volume (moles). Because N₂O is heavier, 85% N₂O by volume is 89.9% by mass; 63.2% by volume is 72.9% by mass.
